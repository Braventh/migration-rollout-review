# Exact trial questionnaire

Use these words verbatim. Do not show the answer key. Square brackets mark facilitator instructions; do not read them aloud as questions.

## After each scenario

1. “Please list every issue you would raise before approving this migration, in the order you found them. For each one, say what you would do next.”
2. “Which issue concerns you most, and why?”
3. “Did you consider anything that you decided was not a real issue? What was it?”
4. [Rollout Desk only] “How actionable is the generated plan on a 1–5 scale, where 1 means ‘not actionable’ and 5 means ‘ready to execute after normal team approval’? Which step in the plan would you actually do first?”
5. [Rollout Desk only] “How much do you trust this output on a 1–5 scale, where 1 means ‘I would reject it outright’, 3 means ‘useful only with selective verification’, and 5 means ‘I would rely on it without checking’? What would you double-check?”
6. [Rollout Desk only] “What did the tool miss, and what did it overstate or present as more important than it is?”
7. “What would you change about the review experience or output you just used?”

## End of session

8. “Compared with your existing workflow, was the Rollout Desk review much worse, worse, about the same, better, or much better? Why?”
9. “Would you use Rollout Desk again for a real migration review: yes, maybe, or no? Why?”
10. “What single change would most improve it for you?”
11. “What kind of migration, team, or release process would it fit best? Where would you avoid relying on it?”
12. “Was there anything confusing, missing, or untrustworthy that we have not already covered?”
13. “Is there anything else you want the owner to know?”

Record exact words where practical, then redact names, employers, customer details, production objects, and secrets before sharing.
